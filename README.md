#### 🔭 Research

My work combines tools and techniques from "classical" mathematical finance---probability theory, stochastic processes, Itô calculus, and so on---with ideas from statistical learning and probabilistic machine learning.
I enjoy working with probabilistic models especially those involving Gaussian processes, mixture models, latent factor models, and situations where expectation–maximisation or variational inference are useful.
Other things I like: kernel methods, reproducing kernel Hilbert spaces, optimisation, and (algebraic) geometry.

My research is almost always guided by some concrete question or application related to quantitative finance. 
Some current interests include...

- Model-based extrapolation of yield curves
- Simultaneous inference of market-implied credit ratings and term structures
- Causal inference and Bayesian statistics
